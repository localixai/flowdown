# Bare links, as cmark-gfm ends them

flowdown's own cases of GitHub's bare links, where GitHub's specification shows less than
cmark-gfm does: which characters at an address's end are let go of. Each is what cmark-gfm
writes, and both of flowdown's parsers are held to it.

## What trails an address

A `;` is let go, alone or with the character reference it ends.

```````````````````````````````` example autolink
https://a.b/c;
.
<p><a href="https://a.b/c">https://a.b/c</a>;</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example autolink
https://a.b/c&hl;
.
<p><a href="https://a.b/c">https://a.b/c</a>&amp;hl;</p>
````````````````````````````````

A `)` is let go while the address closes more brackets than it opens.

```````````````````````````````` example autolink
https://a.b/(c))
.
<p><a href="https://a.b/(c)">https://a.b/(c)</a>)</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example autolink
https://a.b/c).
.
<p><a href="https://a.b/c">https://a.b/c</a>).</p>
````````````````````````````````

Punctuation that ends a sentence is let go, however much of it there is.

```````````````````````````````` example autolink
https://a.b/c?!
.
<p><a href="https://a.b/c">https://a.b/c</a>?!</p>
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example autolink
www.a.b/c...
.
<p><a href="http://www.a.b/c">www.a.b/c</a>...</p>
````````````````````````````````
